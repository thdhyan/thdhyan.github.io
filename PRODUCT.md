# Product: Dhyan Thakkar Portfolio

## Register
brand

## Product Purpose
Personal portfolio for a robotics engineer (MS Robotics, UMN). Showcases projects, publications, and skills to attract hiring managers at robotics companies and academic collaborators. The design IS the signal — it must read as technically sophisticated, not generic.

## Users
- **Primary:** Hiring managers at robotics / AI companies (Boston Dynamics, Agility, Figure, Nvidia Isaac, research labs)
- **Secondary:** Academic collaborators, professors, PhD program admissions
- **Tertiary:** Other robotics engineers evaluating collaboration potential

## Tone
Precise. Confident. Minimal noise. Futuristic but grounded — this isn't a game, it's engineering. No fluff. Every element earns its place.

## Scene sentence
A hiring manager at a robotics company opens this portfolio on a 27-inch monitor in a well-lit office, scanning for signal in under 60 seconds. They've seen a hundred generic dev portfolios today.

## Brand
- Name: Dhyan Thakkar
- Domain: robotics engineering, humanoid / quadruped / UAV / AMR systems
- Palette: deep charcoal backgrounds, rose-purple accents — sophisticated, not neon
- Differentiator: real deployed systems, RL + CV + planning breadth, F-1 OPT eligible

## Anti-references
- Generic blue/white Bootstrap portfolios
- "Creative" portfolios with excessive particle effects
- Dark-mode + neon green "hacker" aesthetic
- Gradient-everything SaaS landing pages

## Strategic principles
1. Signal over decoration — every visual element points to work or credibility
2. Responsiveness is mandatory — mobile hiring is real
3. 3D robot models are the centerpiece, not afterthought
4. Publications and projects carry equal weight
5. Contact must be trivially easy
